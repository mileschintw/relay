Handover's **Coordinator** line now has one strict parser, `relay.parse_coordinator`. `resume.py` (the compact hook) reads the coordinator's worktree and mode through it, and `relay.py ready` uses it to flag a line that is in neither accepted form. Before this, the hook guessed at the line with `worktree (.+?), id `, which failed silently on free-form wording and could pick a previous coordinator named later in the line.

The parser accepts exactly two forms, each at the start of the field and optionally followed by more text:

- the template's `title <t>, worktree <path>, id <id>, mode <normal|unattended|paused>.`;
- `none` (no coordinator holds the plan).

Anything else, a backticked path included, is rejected. `resume.py` then prints nothing, as before. `ready` prints `plan.md line N: Handover > Coordinator is not in the template's shape` under Records, with a hint to rewrite the line in the template's shape or as `none`. The live records' line is already in the template's shape, so `ready` on them prints no such line.

The warning and its hint are ASCII on purpose. `ready` doesn't reconfigure stdout, and `›` or `…` crashed it on a cp950 console.

Setup's plan template now starts the Coordinator field as `none` and shows the template's shape in its text, so a fresh plan passes the check and setup's first `ready` prints nothing about it. GLOSSARY › Handover names the `none` form.
